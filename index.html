ACOUSTIC PHARMACOLOGY.
NEURO-ENGINEERING THE MIND.

QOMA isn't built like ordinary music. It avoids the predictable melodic patterns that keep the mind alert, and works instead with slow-moving sub-bass layers in the 16–40Hz range, gentle amplitude modulation, and monaural beat design, all techniques drawn from research on how the nervous system responds to sound.

We take that research seriously, which also means being honest about its limits: the science of brainwave entrainment is still evolving, and no two minds respond exactly alike. What QOMA offers isn't a medical claim. It's a sound environment engineered with real technique, designed to give your mind permission to slow down.

PURE MATHEMATICS.
ZERO AUDIO FILES.

A true sanctuary never repeats itself. QOMA runs on real-time generative algorithms instead of static audio files, building a different acoustic environment every single session.

Every texture is synthesized live, on your device, using layered noise, careful filtering, and a tuning system built from the ground up for QOMA. Nothing is ever looped, because nothing is ever recorded. No two sessions of QOMA are ever quite the same.

ISOLATION FROM CHAOS.

The modern world is a sea of overstimulation. QOMA acts as a sensory refuge, a space mathematically layered to keep the chaos outside. There is no outside world here. There is only you, your mind, and an infinite digital void.
